Located 550 metres from the swimming center "Aura Waterpark", the smoke-free V Spa & Conference Hotel Tartu offers spacious rooms nearly a 25-minute walk from the Estonian National Museum. This 4-star hotel is a mere 1.9 km from the Tartu Adventure Park and provides guests with a Turkish steam bath and a Jacuzzi.

Location

The hotel is not far from the AHHAA Science Centre and right in the centre of Tartu. Natural attractions in the area include the University of Tartu Botanical Gardens, 750 m from the V Spa & Conference Tartu, and a beach is around 10 minutes' walk away.

Transport

Kesklinn II bus stop is just near the V Spa & Conference.

Nearby Attractions

Visit Anne kanal, 850 m from this accommodation, and feel complete harmony with the nature of Estonia. If you enjoy visiting historical places, the hotel offers good access to the Statue of Kalevipoeg in Tartu, which is merely 0.8 km away.

Rooms

There are 172 rooms at the property, each of them has an iron and ironing board, and an air conditioner, as well as entertainment amenities such as a flat-screen TV with satellite channels. They are appointed with a writing table, and also feature a mini-fridge-bar and coffee/tea making machines. The private bathrooms provide guests with a separate toilet and a shower along with a hair dryer and dressing gowns.

Dining

The lounge bar is ideal for a relaxing drink. Chi is fairly close to V Spa & Conference Hotel and specialises in Chinese cuisine.

Relax & Wellness

A swimming pool and a terrace are available at additional cost as well. You will find a hot tub in the pool area. Onsite wellness options include 11 treatment rooms along with aromatherapy, body wraps, and body treatments.
