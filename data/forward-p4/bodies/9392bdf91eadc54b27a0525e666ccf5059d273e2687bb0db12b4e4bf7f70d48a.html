Offering 214 rooms, the youth The Boc Hostels - City Palma de Mallorca boasts a location about a 5-minute walk from Flea Market. Featuring Wi-Fi in public areas, the hostel is located not far from the Gothic Roman Catholic cathedral "Catedral de Mallorca".

Location

Located 1.2 km from such significant cultural landmarks as the Fundación Bartolomé March Servera, the comfortable hostel is also 2.1 km from Can Pere Antoni Beach. The Boc Hostels - City is also located right near Mercat de Llevant.

Transport

Palma - Estacio Intermodal bus station is 950 m from the Palma de Mallorca hostel.

Nearby Attractions

In proximity to this Palma de Mallorca accommodation, guests will find natural sights, such as the Parc de la Mar, which is around 15 minutes' walk away.

Dining

Serving Japanese dishes, Restaurante japones Primavera is nearly 5 minutes' walk away. This property offers a shared kitchen.

Business

The Boc Hostels - City offers a terrace and a shared lounge to ensure a fun stay in Palma de Mallorca.

Frequently Asked Questions

"What are the check-in and check-out times?"

Check-in is at 14:00, and check-out is at 11:00.

"What is the pet policy at The Boc Hostels - City?"

Pets are allowed upon request, but additional charges may apply, and pet owners should bring a leash, bedding, and food for their pets.

"Is parking available?"

There is no parking available at the hotel, but you can find parking at Plaza de España and Calle de la Unió, both within a 10-minute walk.
