FAQ

Questions, answered

Is ZeroScript really free?

Yes - 100% free with unlimited prompts. Lemonade.gg gives only 4 free prompts then charges $20/month for 100. ZeroScript has no credits and no subscription, because it runs on your own free ChatGPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Gemini, Qwen, Arena or Meta AI chat.

Do I need an API key or to pay for credits?

No. ZeroScript needs no API key, no credits and no subscription. Unlike tools such as Developer Intelligence (which requires your own paid Google Gemini API key) or credit-based tools like Luamotion, ZeroScript runs on your own free AI chat - so it stays genuinely unlimited and free.

Is it safe? It asks to drive my Studio.

It's fully open source (GPL-3.0), so you can read exactly what it does. It connects only to Roblox Studio's official built-in MCP server, needs no account, and sends no telemetry. Everything runs locally on your PC.

Which AI models does it support?

Eight free options: ChatGPT, DeepSeek (recommended), GLM 5.2, Kimi K2.6, Gemini Flash, Qwen, Arena (a multi-model playground that lets you reach many models from one chat - use Direct mode), and Meta AI. You use your own free chat, so there are no API keys or credits to buy.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain English and the AI writes and edits the Luau scripts directly inside Roblox Studio.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows PC, Roblox Studio, Microsoft Edge or Chrome, and a free DeepSeek account. Everything else is in the download.
